Teaching Philosophy

What I Believe

"Every student can learn when I provide clear instruction, encouragement, and meaningful opportunities and support them to practice."

My goal is to create a safe and inclusive classroom where students feel valued and motivated to share their thoughts and do their best. I believe in meeting every learner where they are — especially English learners — by using a wide variety of strategies tailored to their needs.

I value building strong relationships with my students so they feel confident and motivated to succeed. By incorporating their background knowledge and cultural experiences, I work to empower every student and inspire a genuine love of learning that extends beyond the classroom.
